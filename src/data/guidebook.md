# Sunset Ridge Cabin — Guest Guidebook

Welcome to Sunset Ridge Cabin, a two-bedroom A-frame on four wooded acres just
outside Leavenworth, Washington. Everything you need to know about the place is
below.

## Check-In and Check-Out

Check-in is any time after 4:00 PM. Check-out is 11:00 AM.

The cabin uses a keypad lock on the front door. Your four-digit code is sent by
text and email at 2:00 PM on the day of arrival, and it stops working at 11:00 AM
on your check-out day. There is no key and no lockbox — the code is all you need.

Early check-in or late check-out is sometimes possible if nobody is booked next to
your stay. Ask the host the day before and they will let you know.

On the way out, please start the dishwasher, take bagged trash to the bear bin,
turn the thermostat down to 60, and make sure the sliding door to the deck is
locked. You do not need to strip the beds or wash the linens.

## Wifi and Devices

The wifi network is **SunsetRidge-Guest** and the password is **cedarcreek2019**
(all lowercase, no spaces). The same network reaches the loft and the deck, but it
gets weak past the fire pit.

The router is the white box on the shelf under the TV. If the internet drops,
unplug it, wait thirty seconds, plug it back in, and give it about two minutes to
come back up. That fixes it nearly every time.

Cell service is patchy — Verizon gets one or two bars on the deck, AT&T and
T-Mobile usually get nothing. Wifi calling works well and is what most guests use.

The living room TV is a Roku. It is signed into the cabin's own Netflix and Disney+
accounts, so please do not sign out of them. You are welcome to sign in to your own
apps, just remember to log out before you leave.

## House Rules

This is a quiet mountain property and the neighbors are close enough to hear a
party. Quiet hours are 10:00 PM to 8:00 AM.

- The cabin sleeps six. Six is a hard cap, including children and infants.
- No smoking or vaping anywhere on the property, indoors or out. This includes the
  deck and the fire pit area.
- No parties or events.
- Dogs are welcome — up to two, $40 per dog per stay. Please do not let them on the
  furniture or beds, and never leave them alone in the cabin. Cats and other pets
  are not permitted.
- No unregistered overnight guests.
- Please take your shoes off inside. The floors are original fir and scratch easily.

## Parking and Getting Here

There is room for two vehicles on the gravel pad directly in front of the cabin.
Please do not park on the grass or block the turnaround — the propane truck and
emergency vehicles need to get through.

The last half mile of Ridge Access Road is steep gravel and is not plowed by the
county. From roughly November through March you want all-wheel drive and decent
tires. In a heavy snow year chains are worth having in the car. If conditions look
bad, call the host before you attempt the driveway.

GPS is unreliable out here — mapping apps sometimes route you to a locked forest
service gate about a mile past the cabin. Follow the driving directions in your
booking confirmation instead, and watch for the carved wooden bear at the driveway.

There is no EV charging at the cabin. The nearest fast chargers are in downtown
Leavenworth, about 20 minutes away.

## Kitchen

The kitchen is stocked for real cooking: full-size range and oven, refrigerator,
dishwasher, microwave, drip coffee maker, electric kettle, blender, and toaster.
There is a basic set of pots, pans, sheet trays, knives, and mixing bowls, plus
dishes and glassware for eight.

We leave a starter supply of coffee, tea, sugar, salt, pepper, olive oil, dish
soap, dishwasher pods, paper towels, and trash bags. It is meant to get you
started, not to cover a whole week — the grocery store in Leavenworth is the
closest place to restock.

The drip coffee maker takes standard #4 cone filters, which are in the cabinet
directly above it.

Well water runs to the whole cabin. It is tested twice a year and is safe to drink,
though it tastes faintly of minerals. There is a filter pitcher in the fridge if
you prefer.

Please run the dishwasher before you leave, and do not pour grease down the sink —
the cabin is on a septic system.

## Heating and Cooling

The thermostat is on the wall in the hallway between the two bedrooms. It is a
Honeywell dial, not a smart thermostat, so there is no app and no schedule to set.

Turn the dial to your preferred temperature and give the propane furnace a few
minutes to kick in — you will hear it start under the floor. Please keep it between
60 and 74, and turn it down to 60 when you head out for the day or check out.
Propane is expensive up here and the tank is refilled on a schedule.

There is no air conditioning. Summer afternoons can reach the high 80s in the loft,
which is the warmest room in the cabin. The trick most guests use is to open the
windows and the deck door overnight, run the box fans in the closet, and close
everything up again by mid-morning to hold the cool air. Evenings cool off fast.

The wood stove in the living room is decorative only. Please do not light a fire in
it. Firewood stacked outside is for the outdoor fire pit.

## Trash and Recycling

Trash pickup is **Thursday morning**. Please roll the green bin down to the end of
the driveway on Wednesday night and bring it back up when you notice it has been
emptied.

Recycling is the blue bin and goes out on the same Thursday schedule, but only on
the first and third Thursday of the month. Glass is not collected curbside here —
there is a glass drop-off behind the Leavenworth city hall.

All outdoor trash must go in the bear-proof bin beside the shed, with the latch
fully closed. Bears do come through this property, and an unlatched bin is how a
cabin ends up with a bear problem. Never leave trash bags, coolers, or pet food on
the deck overnight.

## Local Recommendations

**Coffee and breakfast.** Argonaut Espresso in town opens at 6:30 AM and makes the
best cortado in the valley. For a full breakfast, South Restaurant does excellent
chilaquiles and is worth the wait on weekends.

**Dinner.** Munchen Haus is the casual pick — bratwurst and beer in a covered beer
garden, no reservations. For something nicer, Watershed Cafe focuses on Pacific
Northwest ingredients and does take reservations, which you will want on a weekend.
Yodelin Broth House is the move on a cold night.

**Hiking.** Icicle Gorge Trail is a flat and genuinely beautiful four-mile loop
that suits almost everyone. Fourth of July Trail is a serious climb with a real
payoff. For the classic Colchuck Lake hike, note that you now need a permit in peak
season and the trailhead lot fills before sunrise.

**With kids.** The Leavenworth Adventure Park has an alpine slide and a ropes
course. Waterfront Park has an easy riverside trail and a swimming beach on the
Wenatchee.

**Wine and beer.** Icicle Ridge Winery does tastings in a log cabin about ten
minutes from here. Doghaus Brewery is dog-friendly and relaxed.

**Groceries.** Dan's Food Market is the full-size grocery store in Leavenworth and
is open until 10:00 PM. The nearest gas is the Chevron on Highway 2.

**Seasonal.** Leavenworth is genuinely mobbed during the Christmas Lighting
Festival on December weekends — plan on slow traffic and book dinner well ahead.
Fall larch season in late September and early October is the local secret.

## Emergency and Contact

For any emergency — fire, medical, or a bear that will not leave — call **911**
first. The cabin address is 14 Ridge Access Road, Leavenworth, WA 98826. Tell
dispatch it is the A-frame with the carved bear at the driveway; that lands better
than the address alone.

Your host is **Dana Whitfield**, reachable at **(509) 555-0142**. Text is fastest
and is answered from 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM. For an urgent problem outside those hours
— no heat, no water, a leak, or you are locked out — call rather than text and it
will go through.

The nearest urgent care is Cascade Medical Center at 817 Commercial Street in
Leavenworth, about 20 minutes away. It has a 24-hour emergency department.

If the power goes out, flashlights are in the drawer under the kitchen island and
the breaker panel is in the utility closet by the back door. Outages of a few hours
are normal in a storm. The water pump runs on electricity, so there will be no
running water until the power returns.

There is a fire extinguisher under the kitchen sink and a first aid kit in the
bathroom closet. Carbon monoxide and smoke detectors are hardwired; if one starts
chirping, tell the host rather than removing the battery.
